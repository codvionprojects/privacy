Privacy Policy

CodVion Internship Program

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CodVion ("CodVion", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your information when you visit intern.codvion.org, apply for the CodVion Internship Program, or take part in it. By using this website or applying for the program, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you apply

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your city and country
  • Your education details, such as your institution and field of study
  • Your skills
  • Your CV or resume and any links you share, such as GitHub or LinkedIn profiles

During the program

  • Attendance and participation records
  • Your task submissions and final project
  • Your messages in our WhatsApp group and email communication with our team
  • Feedback, evaluation and performance records
  • Certificate records, such as your name, field, completion date and certificate ID

Collected automatically

  • Your IP address, browser type, device type and operating system
  • The pages you visit on this website and the time spent on them
  • Your actions on the website, such as clicks and form submissions, collected through the tracking tools described in Section 3

We do not ask for payment or bank details, since the program is free. Please do not send us national ID numbers, passwords or other sensitive documents unless we specifically ask for them and explain why.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Review applications and select interns
  • Run the program, assign tasks, track attendance and review your work
  • Communicate with you about your application, batch updates, schedules and deadlines, through our WhatsApp group and email
  • Evaluate your final project and overall performance, and decide on certificate eligibility
  • Issue certificates and keep records, including the certificate ID, so certificates can be checked later through our verification page
  • Showcase your tasks and final project, as explained in our Terms of Service
  • Send you updates about CodVion internship batches, programs and services
  • Understand how visitors use this website and measure and improve our marketing and advertising, which may include showing our ads to people who have visited our website
  • Keep the program and website safe, and prevent misuse and spam
  • Meet legal requirements

3. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising Tools

This website uses cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help a website work properly and help us understand how it is used. We currently do not show a cookie consent banner on this website. We use the following tools, the same tools used on our main website, codvion.org:

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand which pages are visited and how visitors move through the site. It collects information such as your IP address, device and browser details, and pages viewed. You can learn more in how Google uses data, and you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram): A tool from Meta that collects information about your visits and actions on our website, such as pages viewed, clicks and form submissions. We use it to measure how well our ads perform and to improve and target our advertising. Meta may receive this information together with cookies and device identifiers. You can manage your ad preferences in your Meta ad settings and read the Meta Privacy Policy.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some parts of the website may not work as expected.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell the personal details you send us in your application. Our analytics and advertising tools (Google and Meta) collect technical and usage information as explained in Section 3. We may share information in these situations:

  • Analytics and advertising providers: Google and Meta, through the tools described in Section 3.
  • Our team: CodVion team members and mentors who are involved in reviewing applications and running the program.
  • Service providers: Trusted providers that help us operate the program, such as hosting, email, database, WhatsApp and cloud storage tools. They may use your information only to provide their services to us.
  • Certificate verification: Basic certificate details, such as your name, field and completion date, are shown to anyone who checks a certificate using its certificate ID on our verification page.
  • Showcase of your work: Your tasks and final project may be shown publicly, as described in our Terms of Service.
  • Legal reasons: When the law requires it, or when we need to protect our rights or others.
  • With your consent: In any other case where you agree to it.

5. Other Interns and Program Channels

Since program updates and communication happen in a WhatsApp group, other interns in your batch will be able to see your phone number, name, and what you share in that group. Please share only information you are comfortable with others seeing, and do not share other people's personal information.

6. Third Party Websites and Platforms

This website and the program use or link to third party platforms, such as WhatsApp, GitHub, and LinkedIn. These have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

7. How We Store and Keep Information

Applications and messages sent through our forms are delivered to our email and saved in our database. We keep application, intern and certificate records for record-keeping, for considering you for future batches, and so that certificates can be verified later. We do not delete them on a fixed schedule. You can ask us to delete or anonymize your information at any time (see Section 9).

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access, including limiting access to our email and database to authorized people. However, no website or method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete or anonymize your information, subject to legal or security reasons, or to keeping certificate records for people who have completed the program
  • Opt out of updates and promotional messages, using the unsubscribe option in our emails or by telling us directly
  • Leave the WhatsApp group at any time

We respond to requests to see, correct or delete your information promptly, usually within a few days. You can also opt out of tracking through the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, your Meta ad settings, and your browser's cookie settings, as described in Section 3.

10. Age of Applicants

There is no minimum age requirement to apply for this program. If you are under 18, we encourage you to let a parent or guardian know before you apply and take part.

11. Information Processed Outside Your Country

Some of our service providers, including Google, Meta, WhatsApp, hosting and communication platforms, may be located or store data outside Pakistan. By using this website, you understand that your information may be processed in other countries.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Please check this page regularly.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us:

  • Contact page: codvion.org/contact
  • WhatsApp: +92 321 4086800
