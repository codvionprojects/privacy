CodVion Internship Program

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Welcome to the CodVion Internship Program at intern.codvion.org. These Terms of Service ("Terms") explain the rules that apply when you visit this website, apply for an internship, or take part in the program run by CodVion ("CodVion", "we", "us", "our"). Please read them carefully. By applying for or joining the program, you ("Intern", "you", "your") agree to these Terms.

Program at a Glance

  • Cost: Free. There is no fee to apply, join or receive your certificate.
  • Format: Remote (online)
  • Duration: 3 months
  • Time commitment: Flexible, based on task deadlines
  • Fields: Open to all computer science and IT fields (for example web development, mobile app development, AI and machine learning, IoT and embedded systems), as listed on this website
  • Certificate: Awarded on successful completion of the program requirements, including attendance, tasks, final project and overall performance (see Section 8)

1. About the Program

The CodVion Internship Program is a free, remote, learning focused internship. It gives students and beginners a chance to build practical skills through guided tasks and a final project, and to earn a certificate on completion. The program is designed for training and practice. It is not a job.

2. Eligibility and Applications

  • There is no minimum age requirement to apply.
  • You must provide accurate and complete information in your application. False or misleading information can lead to rejection or removal from the program.
  • Seats are limited. CodVion selects interns at its own discretion, and submitting an application does not guarantee a place.
  • You need your own device, a stable internet connection and the basic software required for your chosen field.

3. Free Internship, No Fees

  • The program is completely free. CodVion does not charge for applying, selection, training or issuing certificates.
  • CodVion will never ask you to send money to be selected or to receive your certificate. If anyone asks you for money in the name of this program, please report it to us immediately using the contact details in Section 18.
  • The internship is unpaid. It does not include a salary, stipend or allowance.

4. Program Structure

  • Duration: The internship runs for 3 months from the start date announced to you.
  • Time commitment: There is no fixed weekly hour requirement. You are expected to give enough time each week to complete your tasks by their deadlines.
  • Field: The program is open to all computer science and IT fields. You will be placed in a field based on your application and the seats available.
  • Communication: Tasks, guidance, updates and feedback are shared through a WhatsApp group and email.
  • Training projects only: Interns work only on training and practice projects prepared for the program. You will not be given access to real client projects, client data or confidential client information.

5. No Employment Relationship

Taking part in the program does not make you an employee, contractor or representative of CodVion. The internship does not guarantee a job, paid work, a future internship, or a recommendation letter. CodVion may, at its own choice, offer opportunities to interns who perform well, but it is under no obligation to do so.

6. Intern Responsibilities

As an intern, you agree to:

  • Take part actively and complete assigned tasks by their deadlines
  • Inform the team in advance if you cannot meet a deadline
  • Communicate respectfully and professionally with the team and other interns, including in the WhatsApp group
  • Look after your own device, internet connection, accounts and data
  • Submit only your own original work

7. Attendance, Tasks and Final Project

  • Attendance: You must attend at least 80% of scheduled sessions, meetings or check ins during the program.
  • Tasks: All assigned tasks must be submitted by their deadlines. Late or missing tasks may affect your eligibility for the certificate.
  • Final project: Every intern must complete and submit a final project at the end of the program. The CodVion team reviews it before the certificate is approved.
  • Overall performance: The quality and consistency of your work, participation and conduct throughout the program are also taken into account when deciding certificate eligibility.
  • Original work: Your submissions must be your own work. Copying another person's work, copying from another intern, or presenting someone else's work as yours (plagiarism) is not allowed. You may use AI tools to help you learn and complete tasks, but you must understand your work and be able to explain it if we ask you.

8. Certificate

  • A certificate is awarded when you have completed the full 2 month duration, met the 80% attendance requirement, submitted all assigned tasks, completed your final project, and maintained satisfactory overall performance throughout the program.
  • The certificate is issued as a digital PDF or image. It carries the name you gave in your application, so please make sure your name is spelled correctly.
  • Each certificate has a unique ID that can be checked through our certificate verification page, so employers or institutions can confirm it is genuine.
  • CodVion may refuse, withhold or cancel a certificate if the requirements are not met, or in cases of misconduct, plagiarism, or false information.
  • The certificate confirms your participation in and completion of this training program. It is not a degree, diploma, license or accredited qualification.

9. Intellectual Property and Showcasing Your Work

  • Your work: You keep ownership of the original work you create during the program, including your tasks and final project.
  • License to CodVion: By taking part in the program, you agree that CodVion may review, evaluate, and showcase your tasks and final project (for example on our website, portfolio or social media) for educational and promotional purposes, without needing to ask you separately each time and without attributing the work to your name.
  • Program materials: Task briefs, learning materials, templates, designs, documents and branding provided by CodVion remain the property of CodVion. You may use them for your own learning, but you may not copy, share publicly, sell or redistribute them.
  • Third party material: Third party content and open source software remain under their own licenses.

10. Confidentiality

Any non public information shared with you during the program, including internal communications, unpublished materials, and the personal details of other participants or team members, must be kept confidential and used only for the purpose of the program.

11. Code of Conduct

You agree to:

  • Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, discrimination, abuse, threats or offensive behavior are not tolerated.
  • Not share spam, harmful content, malware or anything unlawful in the WhatsApp group, email or any other program channel
  • Not attempt to access accounts, systems or data that you are not authorized to use
  • Not misuse the CodVion name, logo or brand

If the Code of Conduct is broken, CodVion may issue a warning, suspend your participation, or remove you from the program.

12. Use of CodVion's Name

You may say that you took part in the CodVion Internship Program, and you may share your certificate. You may not claim to be a CodVion employee or representative, speak on behalf of CodVion, or state that you worked on client projects, since the program consists of training and practice projects only.

13. Withdrawal, Removal and Program Changes

  • Withdrawal: You may leave the program at any time, with no advance notice required. If you leave before completing the requirements, no certificate will be issued.
  • Removal: CodVion may remove an intern for extended inactivity, repeated missed deadlines, misconduct, plagiarism, or false information.
  • Program changes: CodVion may update the program schedule, tasks, fields, or format, or postpone or cancel a batch when needed. If we cancel or reschedule a batch, we will inform the affected applicants or interns.

14. Personal Information and Privacy

When you apply and take part, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, contact details, education, skills, and submitted work. We use it to review applications, run the program, communicate with you, evaluate your work, and issue and keep records of certificates. Please see our Privacy Policy for full details.
