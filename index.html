CodVion Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Welcome to CodVion. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website codvion.org or engage our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you contact us or use our services:

  • Personal Details: Your Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  • Project Information: Logins, passwords, and any other credentials or materials necessary to complete your project.
  • Website Usage Data: We use Cookies, Google Analytics, Cloudflare, and similar tools to monitor website traffic, enhance security, and improve user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Deliver the web development, app development, SEO, and other digital services you requested.
  • Communicate with you regarding your project and provide post delivery support.
  • Retain your project credentials like logins and passwords to offer ongoing support and maintenance.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. However, we reserve the right to use the completed projects such as website designs, apps, or case studies in our company portfolio and marketing materials to showcase our work.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal and project data from unauthorized access or misuse.

5. Your Rights

If you wish to update or request the deletion of your personal data, you can contact us at info@codvion.org.



CodVion Internship Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy applies to applicants and participants of the CodVion Internship Program at intern.codvion.org.

1. Information We Collect

When you apply for or participate in the CodVion Internship Program, we collect:

  • Application Data: Your Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and CV or Resume.
  • Performance Data: Your task submissions, assignments, and attendance records during the program.

2. How We Use Intern Data

We use this information to:

  • Evaluate your application and manage your participation in the internship.
  • Track your progress and determine your eligibility for a certificate.

3. Data Retention and Verification

We retain intern data including names, contact details, and performance records permanently, for as long as CodVion is established. This allows us to maintain academic records and provide a permanent verification system for your issued certificates.

4. Data Sharing

Intern data is used strictly for internal evaluation and certificate generation. We do not sell or share intern data with external parties.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy related inquiries, please contact us via WhatsApp at +92 321 4086800 or email us at info@codvion.org.
